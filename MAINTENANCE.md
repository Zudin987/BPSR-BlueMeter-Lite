# Maintenance Status

BlueMeter Lite is a small community project maintained on a best-effort basis.

The source remains public, but routine support, device-specific diagnosis, and
feature requests are not guaranteed. Critical compatibility, security, or
low-risk reliability updates may be published when practical.

Blue Protocol: Star Resonance updates can change the network protocol and stop
the meter from working. Compatibility fixes are not guaranteed or bound to a
specific response time.
